    Why I made Hijab Match

    Growing up, I always wore a black hijab. It went with everything, but if I'm honest, it was also a way to hide.

    I made Hijab Match for girls who want to wear more than black and feel confident doing it. It helps you find the hijab in your own closet that pulls an outfit together, and it might suggest a colour you wouldn't have tried.

    How it works

    1. Photograph each of your hijabs once and tap the fabric to save its colour.
    2. Photograph your outfit and tap up to 3 pieces.
    3. Your hijabs are ranked 3, 2 or 1 stars, with the reason each one works.

    Your photos stay on your phone

    Everything happens on your device. There's no account, and no photo is ever uploaded.
