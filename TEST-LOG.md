Shanzay Chaudhry, October to November 2026. My raw notes from testing, as I wrote them. The cleaned-up write-up is in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

Goal: for at least 80% of outfits, the app's top pick is one I'd actually wear.

## Verdict scale

**My choice** and **Would consider** both count as a pass toward the 80% goal.

| Verdict | Meaning | Counts as a pass? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My choice | It's the hijab I'd have picked, or just as good | Yes |
| Would consider | Not my first pick, but I'd happily wear it | Yes |
| Wrong | I wouldn't wear it with this outfit | No |
