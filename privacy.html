Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version: Hijab Match doesn't collect your data. Your photos and your closet stay on your phone. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

What the app stores

For each hijab you save, the app keeps a small square picture (150 pixels), its colour, its name and a few settings. These are stored in your browser's own storage on your device and nowhere else.

Outfit photos are never stored. They're gone when you close the app.

What leaves your phone

No photos and no closet data. All colour reading and matching happens on your device.

Like any website, two services receive a basic request when you open the app, which includes your IP address and browser details:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's font. See Google Fonts privacy.

Deleting your data

Delete a hijab from your closet in the app, or remove everything by clearing website data for shanzayc.github.io in your browser settings. On iPhone: Settings, Apps, Safari, Advanced, Website Data.

There's no copy of your closet anywhere else, so once it's cleared, it can't be recovered.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Questions
